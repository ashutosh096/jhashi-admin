ADMINISTRATION · BACKGROUND BRIEF JHANSI · BUNDELKHAND · 2026 · NOT FOR DIRECT PUBLICATION

Across 123 years of rainfall and 73 years of temperature records, the data describe a district in which the pre-monsoon season is becoming progressively drier in half its blocks, and progressively hotter in every block, without exception.

Block-Level Pattern
Drying + Warming (4 blocks) Moth, Bamaur, Chirgaon, Gursarai. Rainfall declining; temperatures rising.
Smallest block in this group Chirgaon (central). Drying trend persists despite small area.

What the data describe

The report documents two parallel signals operating across the district during the months when water is already scarcest. Half the blocks are receiving progressively less rainfall in the pre-monsoon season. All eight blocks are experiencing rising air temperatures, with the largest gains in short-duration heat extremes that drive evaporation. Where these two signals overlap — in Moth, Bamaur, Chirgaon and Gursarai — the pre-monsoon water deficit is widening fastest.

"The data describe a self-reinforcing pattern: lower rainfall input combined with higher evaporative demand. The pre-monsoon water deficit in Jhansi is not an event. It is a trend the record has captured for over a century." — From the underlying report, ClimAgro Analytics 2026
